Percent Conversions

Change between percents, decimals, and fractions. Remember: to turn a percent into a decimal, divide by 100 (move the decimal point two places left).

Percent Applications

Every percent problem has a Base (the whole), a Part (the piece), and a Rate (the percent). Some problems give you a new total after a percent increase or decrease and ask for the original amount.

Statistics

Mean (average), median (middle value once sorted), mode (most frequent value), and weighted mean (average that accounts for how often each value occurs).

Wages & Salary

Overtime pay, piecework vs. hourly pay, base salary plus commission, and converting an annual salary into a weekly paycheck.
